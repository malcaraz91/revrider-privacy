Rev Rider: Moto Hill Climb — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Rev Rider: Moto Hill Climb (package name com.alcarazlabs.revrider), published by Alcaraz Labs (“We”, “Us”, “Our”), handles information when You use it, and explains Your privacy rights.

1. Definitions

  • Application or Service: the Rev Rider: Moto Hill Climb game.
  • Device: any device that can run the Application, such as a cell phone or tablet.
  • Personal Data: any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data: data collected automatically through the use of the Service (for example IP address, device identifiers or diagnostic data).
  • You: the individual using the Application.

2. Information We collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any Personal Data ourselves. The Application has no accounts or registration, and it does not ask for Your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal or sensitive data.

Information collected by our advertising provider: the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are loaded and shown, Google may automatically collect Usage Data from Your Device, such as Your advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device type, operating system version, app version, and information about how You interact with ads and the Application. Google may use technologies similar to cookies and device identifiers for this. We do not receive this data and cannot access it. See section 6.

Apart from advertising, the Application does not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services. The Application needs an internet connection only to load ads.

3. Data stored on Your Device

To work properly, the Application saves the following only on Your own Device: Your progress (unlocked stages, completed stages and Your best distance on each stage), Your coins and gems, the motorcycles You own, the one You have equipped and its upgrades, Your tutorial progress, and Your settings (volume, mute, vibration, air control sensitivity and brake zone size). This data is not sent to Us and We cannot access it.

The Application can make Your Device vibrate during play. This is controlled locally by the vibration setting and does not involve sending any data.

4. Use of information

The data saved on Your Device is used only to run the game for You: showing Your progress, records, garage and settings. We do not use it for profiling or analytics, and We do not contact You through the Application. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under section 6.

5. Permissions

The Application only requests the permissions needed to run the game and show ads: internet access (to load ads) and vibration (an optional game effect). It does not request access to Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to show ads, which helps keep the game free. The Application shows interstitial ads (for example between runs or after finishing a stage) and rewarded ads (video ads that You choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as extra coins or a second chance). Google acts as an independent service provider and handles the data it collects under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Purposes: serving and measuring ads, limiting how often You see an ad, and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Personalized ads and consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Application asks for Your consent through a Google-certified consent message before showing personalized ads. You can review or change Your choice at any time in the Application’s settings, where it applies to You.
  • Your choices: You can reset or delete Your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Your Device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads), and manage Google’s ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com. Opting out does not remove ads; they just become less relevant.

7. Sharing and disclosure of information

  • Service Providers: data from Your Device used for advertising is collected by and shared with Google (AdMob), as described in section 6. We do not otherwise sell, rent or share Personal Data with any third party or affiliate, because We do not collect it.
  • Legal requirements: We hold no Personal Data about You, so there is none to disclose to authorities. If this ever changes, We would disclose it only when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: if the Application is transferred to another owner, this policy (or an updated one) will keep applying.

8. Retention and deletion of data

We keep no Personal Data on our servers, so there is nothing for Us to retain or delete. The data saved on Your Device stays there until You remove it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Your Device settings or by uninstalling the Application (this erases Your progress, coins, gems, motorcycles, upgrades, records and settings).

If You have device backups turned on, Android may include the app’s saved data in Your Google backup. That backup is managed by Google and Your Device settings, not by Us. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s own policies; You can manage it with the controls in section 6.

9. Your rights

You are in control of the data in the Application: You can delete it as described above. For advertising data held by Google, You can exercise Your choices and rights (such as access, deletion or opting out of personalization) through Your Device settings and Google’s tools described in section 6. Since We do not hold Your Personal Data, requests to Us cannot involve our systems, but You may still contact Us at any time with questions or requests, and We will respond.

10. International transfers

The data saved by the Application stays on Your Device and is not processed on our servers. Data collected by Google for advertising may be processed in the countries where Google operates, under the safeguards that Google describes in its privacy policy.

11. Security

The data saved by the Application stays in the app’s private storage on Your Device, protected by the security features of Your operating system. Because the Application itself does not transmit Your game data to any server of ours, there is no server of ours that could expose it. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, so We cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children’s and minors’ privacy

The Application is a general-audience game. It is not directed to, and We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from, anyone under the age of 16, and We do not intentionally show personalized ads to children. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us and We will take the necessary steps.

13. Links

The Application and its store listing contain a link to this Privacy Policy, which opens in Your Device’s browser. Ads shown by Google AdMob may lead to advertisers’ websites or app stores; if You follow such a link, We recommend reviewing that site’s own privacy policy, as We have no control over it.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date at the top. Changes are effective when they are posted, so please review this page periodically.

15. Contact Us

If You have any questions about this Privacy Policy, You can contact Us by email: apkmiguelapk@gmail.com

Los datos guardados en Tu Dispositivo se usan solo para que el juego funcione para Ti: mostrar Tu progreso, récords, garaje y ajustes. La Aplicación solo solicita los permisos necesarios para funcionar y mostrar anuncios: acceso a internet (para cargar anuncios) y vibración (un efecto opcional del juego). No solicita acceso a Tu ubicación, contactos, fotos, cámara, micrófono ni archivos.
